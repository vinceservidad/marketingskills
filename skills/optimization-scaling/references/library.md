The owned scaling frameworks, playbooks, and templates. The method references linked from `SKILL.md` govern; the frameworks below are one-page summaries of them for quick orientation. When a summary and a method reference appear to differ, follow the method reference.

- [Google Ads scaling](../_shared/playbooks/google-ads-scaling.md) — which controlled Google Ads step is justified.

## Templates

- [Scale readiness scorecard](../_shared/templates/scale-readiness.md) — gate-by-gate verdict record.
- [Scaling economics worksheet](../_shared/templates/scaling-economics.md) — formulas, cost basis, sensitivity.
- [Scaling hypothesis brief](../_shared/templates/scaling-hypothesis.md) and [scaling experiment brief](../_shared/templates/scaling-experiment.md) — one interpretable change and its test design.
- [Scaling change plan](../_shared/templates/scaling-change-plan.md) — exact step, exposure, guardrails, approval, rollback.
- [Scaling portfolio review](../_shared/templates/scaling-portfolio-review.md) — entity classification for reallocation.
- [Scaling decision log](../_shared/templates/scaling-decision-log.md) — decisions, proof level, and replication status.
- [De-scaling plan](../_shared/templates/de-scaling-plan.md) and [recovery verification](../_shared/templates/recovery-verification.md) — reducing exposure and confirming recovery.

A filled template is a decision record, not authorization. Live changes still require explicit approval.
