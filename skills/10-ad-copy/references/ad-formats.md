# Ad formats: what to confirm before writing

Platform limits and policies change. Confirm the current values in the platform's own help pages or the ad account before writing. Record where each was checked. If you can't check, write "to confirm".

| Platform | Fields to confirm | Policy areas to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search (responsive search ads) | number and length of headlines and descriptions; path fields | trademarks, superlatives, misleading claims |
| Meta (Facebook and Instagram) | primary text (visible length before "more"), headline, description | personal-attribute language ("Are you struggling with…?"), before/after claims, special ad categories |
| LinkedIn | intro text, headline, description | professional claims, data use |
| TikTok | caption length, on-screen text | claims, music rights |

## Angles

- **Outcome:** what they get.
- **Problem:** what stops now.
- **Mechanism:** why this works.
- **Proof:** who it worked for.
- **Identity:** for people like you.
- **Objection:** the doubt, answered.

## SLAP: Stop, Look, Act, Purchase

Use SLAP as the shape of one ad. Stop: the first line or hook halts the scroll. Look: the next lines hold attention with the one reason to click. Act: one call to action. Purchase: the click lands on a destination that carries the same promise toward the purchase. Check each ad for all four steps before testing variants.

## Testing

Change one element per variant (the hook, the angle or the call to action) and decide in advance the metric and the minimum data that settles it.
